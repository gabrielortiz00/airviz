About

3D view of Class B, C and D airspace across the US, including Alaska, Hawaii and the territories. Hover a volume to see its name and altitudes. Drag to pan, right-drag or Ctrl-drag to tilt and rotate.

Airspace from the FAA NASR 28-day subscription. Where airspace areas overlap, ownership follows the FAA legal descriptions. Not shown yet: Class E, special use airspace, TFRs and part-time airspace hours.

Basemap from OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors. Terrain from AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen). Source code on GitHub.
